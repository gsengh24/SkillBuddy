How matching works

Every request starts with understanding what you're asking for. Then four steps narrow everyone down to a few.

You write a few sentences. A model reads them and pulls out what you offer, what you're looking for and what you're into. You review the result and correct it. Your own words are always kept.

Model reads your text once

Four parts of every profile become vectors: who you are, what you offer, what you seek and what you're into. A vector search and a keyword search narrow everyone to a few hundred people, after blocks and availability are applied.

4 facets · 384 dimensions

Candidates are scored on fit and on whether the match works both ways. Small penalties stop the same people being shown to everyone, but they never lift a poor fit over a good one. Anyone below the quality floor is dropped.

Weights applied in code, not by the model

A model reads up to fifteen candidates and scores how well each meets your need and how much you share. Anyone with a conflict is dropped. The rest come with a reason both of you see. If nobody fits, we say so.
