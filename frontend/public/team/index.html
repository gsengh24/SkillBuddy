Why Cynergi exists

At college you meet collaborators by accident: whoever sits next to you, whoever's in the group chat. Cynergi asks what you're trying to do first, finds a few people with a reason for each, and contacts nobody until both of you say yes. Matching runs on free models that don't train on your words, and no AI ever reads your chats.
